What Our Crew Does When We Arrive

  1. Safety check.We look for electrical risks and sagging ceilings before anyone walks in.
  2. Confirm the source is stopped.If the water is still running, we help you shut it off.
  3. Extract standing water.Powerful extractors remove water from carpet, pad, tile, and concrete.
  4. Map hidden moisture.Meters and thermal cameras show where water went inside walls and under floors.
  5. Move and protect contents.Furniture is lifted on blocks or moved out of the wet area.
  6. Set up drying.Air movers and dehumidifiers go in right away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a shop vac instead?

A shop vac helps with small spills. It can't pull water out of carpet pad, drywall, or subfloor. Deeper water needs professional extraction and drying.

How long does water removal take?

Extraction usually takes a few hours. Drying afterward takes several days.

Is it safe to walk on wet carpet?

Try to avoid it. Walking pushes water deeper into the pad and can spread dirty water. Stay off it if there are outlets or cords nearby.

Do you remove water from apartments and upper floors?

Yes. Portable equipment lets us work in apartments, condos, and second floor units.

Should I turn on my AC or heat?

Running the AC can help pull humidity out of the air. Avoid high heat, which can speed up mold growth.
