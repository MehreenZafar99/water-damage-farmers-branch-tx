If the Ceiling Is Sagging

A sagging ceiling can collapse without warning. Do this:

  1. Move people, pets, and furniture out from under it
  2. Put a bucket or tarp below the sag
  3. Turn off power to lights and fans in that ceiling
  4. Do not press on the bulge
  5. Reach us so we can drain it and assess it safely

How We Restore Water Damaged Ceilings

  1. Find and stop the source, whether it's the roof, a pipe, a bathroom, or the AC
  2. Check the space above the ceiling with thermal imaging
  3. Remove wet insulation and damaged drywall
  4. Dry the ceiling cavity and framing
  5. Install new drywall, match the texture, and paint

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I just paint over a water stain?

Only once the leak is fixed and the drywall is fully dry. Otherwise the stain comes back and mold can grow behind the paint.

Can you match my ceiling texture?

Yes. We match common textures like popcorn, knockdown, and orange peel as closely as possible.

Is a ceiling stain an emergency?

A growing or dripping stain is. An old dry stain still needs checking, but it can usually wait a day.

Why is my ceiling wet under an upstairs bathroom?

Common causes are a failed toilet wax ring, a cracked shower pan, or a leaking tub drain.
