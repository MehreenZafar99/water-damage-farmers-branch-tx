## Shut It Off: Gas vs Electric

**For every water heater:**
1. Close the cold water valve on the pipe at the top of the tank
2. If you can't find it or it won't turn, shut off the main water valve

**Gas water heater:**
3. Turn the gas control knob to "Off" or "Pilot"
4. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas company

**Electric water heater:**
3. Switch off the water heater breaker at the electrical panel
4. Never touch the unit while standing in water

## Water Heater Health Check

Run through this once a year:

- [ ] Find the age on the label. Most tanks last about 8 to 12 years
- [ ] Look for rust on the tank, fittings, and floor around it
- [ ] Check for moisture or drips under the tank
- [ ] Listen for popping or rumbling sounds from sediment buildup
- [ ] Watch for rusty or cloudy hot water
- [ ] Make sure a drain pan sits under the tank, especially in attics and closets
- [ ] Test the relief valve by lifting the lever briefly
- [ ] Flush the tank to clear sediment

North Texas water is fairly hard, so sediment builds up faster. That shortens tank life if it's never flushed.

## Where the Tank Sits Changes the Damage

| Location | What gets damaged |
|---|---|
| Garage | Concrete floor, drywall along the walls, stored boxes |
| Hall or utility closet | Carpet, baseboards, walls in nearby rooms |
| Attic | Ceiling drywall, insulation, and rooms below |
| Upstairs closet | Subfloor and the ceiling below |

Attic and upstairs tanks cause the biggest messes because water moves down through the house.

## Our Cleanup Process

1. Extract water from floors and carpet
2. Check nearby walls and the ceiling below for hidden moisture
3. Remove wet baseboards, pad, and damaged drywall
4. Dry the area with air movers and dehumidifiers
5. Restore walls and floors once readings are dry

A plumber replaces the water heater. We take care of the water damage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a water heater leak covered by insurance?
A sudden tank burst is often covered for the water damage. The water heater itself usually is not.

### Is water heater water dangerous?
It's usually clean water, but it can be very hot at first. Let it cool and stay clear of electrical parts.

### Why is water pooling but the tank looks fine?
The leak may be from the relief valve, a fitting, or the drain valve. It still needs a plumber and quick drying.

### How long does cleanup take?
Extraction takes a few hours. Drying usually takes 3 to 5 days.
