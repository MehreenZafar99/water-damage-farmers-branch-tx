## How a Hail Storm Leak Usually Unfolds

**Storm night.** Hail cracks or knocks loose shingles. Rain gets under them, but you don't see anything inside yet.

**Day 1 to 2.** Water soaks the roof deck and drips onto attic insulation. The insulation holds it like a sponge.

**Day 2 to 4.** A faint stain appears on a ceiling. It may be far from where the roof is damaged.

**Next rain.** The stain grows or starts to drip. The drywall above may begin to sag.

**About a week later.** A musty smell shows up as mold starts growing on wet insulation and drywall.

The earlier you catch it, the smaller the repair. Check your attic within a day or two of any hail.

## What to Photograph for Your Insurance Claim

| Outside | Inside |
|---|---|
| Missing, cracked, or dented shingles (from the ground or a safe spot) | Every ceiling stain, with a wide shot and a close up |
| Dents in gutters, vents, and downspouts | Wet insulation in the attic |
| Damaged window screens and siding | Water at window frames and sills |
| Hail on the ground, with something for scale | Damaged furniture, rugs, and belongings |
| Fallen branches on the roof | Date stamped photos of drips or buckets catching water |

Take photos before any cleanup or repairs start.

## What We Do After a Storm

1. Put a temporary tarp on the roof if water is still getting in
2. Check the attic and ceilings with thermal imaging
3. Remove soaked insulation and damaged ceiling drywall
4. Dry the attic, framing, and wall cavities
5. Share photos and moisture logs you can send to your insurer
6. Repair drywall, texture, and paint once dry

Your roofer handles the permanent roof repair.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How soon after a hail storm should I check for leaks?
Within a day or two. Check the attic first, since water often shows up there before any ceiling stain.

### Does insurance cover hail damage?
Hail is often covered in Texas homeowners policies. Many have a separate wind and hail deductible, so check yours.

### Can I wait for the roofer before calling you?
Don't wait if water is already inside. We can tarp the roof and start drying while you schedule the roofer.

### Why is my ceiling stain far from the damaged part of the roof?
Water runs along the roof deck and rafters before it drips down.
