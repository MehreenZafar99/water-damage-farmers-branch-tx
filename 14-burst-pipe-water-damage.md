# Burst Pipe Water Damage in Farmers Branch

A burst pipe can release hundreds of gallons in an hour. The water doesn't stay where you can see it. It runs inside walls, under flooring, and down to the rooms below. We remove the water, find where it spread, and dry your Farmers Branch property the right way.

## Burst Pipe Shutoff Checklist

- [ ] **Find the main valve.** Look near the front foundation, in the garage, or at the meter box near the curb.
- [ ] **Turn it clockwise** until it stops.
- [ ] **Open the lowest faucet** in the house to drain remaining water from the lines.
- [ ] **Turn off the water heater** if the leak is on the hot side.
- [ ] **Shut off power** to rooms with standing water if you can do it safely.
- [ ] **Call a plumber** to fix the pipe and call us for the water damage.

## Pipe Types Found in Farmers Branch Homes

The age of your home tells you a lot about what's inside the walls.

| Pipe type | Common in | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| Galvanized steel | Many homes built before the 1970s | Rusts from the inside out. Leaks and bursts get more likely with age |
| Copper | Homes from the 1960s onward | Long lasting, but joints and pinhole leaks can fail |
| Polybutylene (gray plastic) | Some homes from the late 1970s to mid 1990s | Known to crack and fail without warning |
| CPVC | Homes and remodels from the 1980s onward | Can turn brittle over time |
| PEX | Newer homes and remodels | Flexible and freeze resistant, but fittings can fail |

## Signs of a Hidden Burst Inside a Wall

A pipe doesn't always burst where you can see it. Watch for:

- A sudden drop in water pressure
- Your water meter still spinning with every tap off
- A higher than normal water bill
- Wet drywall, peeling paint, or a bulge in the wall
- The sound of running water when nothing is on
- Warped baseboards or damp carpet along a wall

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is burst pipe damage covered by insurance?
A sudden burst is often covered. Damage from a slow leak or a lack of maintenance often isn't. See our [insurance guide](/water-damage-insurance-claims/).

### Do you fix the pipe?
A licensed plumber repairs the pipe. We handle the water removal, drying, and restoration.

### Why does the ceiling below the leak look worse than the leak area?
Water follows gravity. It collects on the ceiling below and soaks the drywall there.

### How long does it take to dry after a burst pipe?
Usually 3 to 5 days. Wall cavities and hardwood can take longer.
