## Sewage Safety Checklist

Until our crew arrives:

- [ ] Keep everyone, especially kids and pets, out of the area
- [ ] Stop using all sinks, toilets, showers, and appliances that drain
- [ ] Turn off the HVAC system so it doesn't spread odors and germs
- [ ] Turn off power to the area if you can do it from a dry spot
- [ ] Don't try to clean it with a mop or shop vac
- [ ] Wear gloves and boots if you must enter
- [ ] Wash hands and clothes after any contact
- [ ] Take photos for your insurance

## What Causes Sewage Backups Here

| Cause | Why it's common in Farmers Branch |
|---|---|
| Tree roots in the sewer line | Mature trees in established neighborhoods push roots into older pipe joints |
| Aging cast iron or clay pipes | Many homes built in the 1950s to 1970s still have original drain lines that crack and collapse |
| Grease buildup | Kitchen grease hardens inside the line and blocks flow |
| Items flushed down toilets | Wipes and paper towels don't break down |
| Heavy rain | Storms can overload sewer lines and push water back toward homes |
| Soil movement | Shifting clay soil can crack or sag drain lines under the slab |

## What Has to Go and What Can Stay

Sewage is Category 3 water. Porous materials that soak it up can't be fully cleaned.

| Removed and thrown out | Cleaned and disinfected |
|---|---|
| Carpet and pad | Tile and sealed concrete |
| Wet drywall and insulation | Metal and glass |
| Upholstered furniture | Solid wood furniture that didn't absorb water |
| Mattresses and bedding | Toilets, tubs, and sinks |
| Particleboard cabinets | Hard plastic items |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I stay home during sewage cleanup?
If the backup is contained to one area and you can avoid it, often yes. For large backups or if anyone in the home has a weak immune system, staying elsewhere is safer.

### Is sewage backup covered by insurance?
Many standard policies don't cover it unless you added water backup or sewer backup coverage. Check your policy.

### Why does sewage cleanup cost more than other water damage?
It needs protective gear, containment, disinfection, and removal of porous materials.

### How do I know if the city sewer or my line is the problem?
If neighbors have the same issue, it may be the city main. If it's only your property, it's likely your line. A plumber can check with a camera.
