# Ceiling Water Damage Repair in Farmers Branch

A water stain on the ceiling is a message. Something above it is leaking, and the drywall is holding the water for now. We find the source, dry the space above your ceiling, and repair the damage in Farmers Branch homes, townhomes, and buildings.

## What Your Ceiling Is Telling You

| What you see | What it usually means |
|---|---|
| Yellow or brown ring | An old or slow leak that dried and came back |
| Fresh wet spot that keeps growing | An active leak right now |
| Bubbling or peeling paint | Water trapped between paint and drywall |
| Sagging or a soft bulge | Drywall heavy with water. It may fall |
| Stain under a bathroom | Leaking toilet seal, shower pan, or tub drain |
| Stain near an exterior wall | Roof edge, gutter, or window leak |
| Stain under the AC closet or attic unit | Clogged condensate line or overflowing drain pan |
| Dark spots or fuzzy growth | Mold growing on the drywall |

---

## If the Ceiling Is Sagging

A sagging ceiling can collapse without warning. Do this:

1. Move people, pets, and furniture out from under it
2. Put a bucket or tarp below the sag
3. Turn off power to lights and fans in that ceiling
4. Do not press on the bulge
5. Call us so we can drain it and assess it safely

## Repair or Replace?

| Can often be repaired | Usually needs replacing |
|---|---|
| Small stain on dry, firm drywall | Drywall that is soft, crumbly, or sagging |
| Clean water leak dried within a day or two | Drywall wet for several days |
| Surface paint damage only | Drywall with mold growth |
| Textured ceiling with minor staining | Drywall soaked with dirty water |

## How We Restore Water Damaged Ceilings

1. Find and stop the source, whether it's the roof, a pipe, a bathroom, or the AC
2. Check the space above the ceiling with thermal imaging
3. Remove wet insulation and damaged drywall
4. Dry the ceiling cavity and framing
5. Install new drywall, match the texture, and paint

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I just paint over a water stain?
Only once the leak is fixed and the drywall is fully dry. Otherwise the stain comes back and mold can grow behind the paint.

### Can you match my ceiling texture?
Yes. We match common textures like popcorn, knockdown, and orange peel as closely as possible.

### Is a ceiling stain an emergency?
A growing or dripping stain is. An old dry stain still needs checking, but it can usually wait a day.

### Why is my ceiling wet under an upstairs bathroom?
Common causes are a failed toilet wax ring, a cracked shower pan, or a leaking tub drain.
