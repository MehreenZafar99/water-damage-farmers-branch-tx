Quick answer: Most small, clean water jobs in one room cost a few thousand dollars. Larger jobs, dirty water, and sewage cost more. The exact price depends on how big the area is, what type of water it is, what materials got wet, and how fast drying starts.

What Drives the Price

FactorWhy it matters
Size of the wet areaMore square footage means more extraction time and more drying equipment
Type of waterClean water is simpler. Gray water and sewage need cleaning, disinfection, and removal of porous materials
Materials affectedCarpet, drywall, hardwood, and cabinets each dry and repair differently
How long water satWater left for days spreads further and raises the chance of mold
Hidden moistureWater inside walls or under floors can require opening walls or lifting flooring
Repairs neededDrywall, paint, flooring, and trim replacement add to the total

The Three Water Categories

CategorySource examplesWhat it means for cost
Category 1 (clean)Broken supply line, burst pipe, sink overflowLowest cost. Many materials can be dried in place
Category 2 (gray)Washing machine or dishwasher discharge, toilet overflow with urineModerate cost. Needs cleaning and some material removal
Category 3 (black)Sewage backup, outside floodwaterHighest cost. Porous materials are usually removed

Clean water can turn into gray water in about 48 hours if it isn't dried. Fast action keeps the job smaller.

How to Keep Your Cost Down

  1. Shut off the water fast.Less water means less to remove.
  2. Call right away.Drying in the first 24 to 48 hours saves more material.
  3. Move belongings out of the wet areaso they don't absorb water.
  4. Don't wait to see if it dries.Hidden moisture turns into mold and bigger repairs.
  5. Check your insurance early.Many sudden leaks are covered. See our insurance claims guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you give an estimate before starting?

Yes. After the inspection we explain the scope and cost before work begins.

Why does sewage cleanup cost more?

Sewage carries bacteria. Carpet, pad, and wet drywall usually must be removed, and the area needs disinfection.

Does insurance cover the cost?

Sudden and accidental damage is often covered. Slow leaks and outside flooding usually are not unless you have flood coverage.

Is it cheaper to dry it myself?

It can look cheaper at first. But fans often leave water inside walls and under floors, which can lead to mold and much higher repair costs later.

How is drying equipment priced?

Most companies price drying by the number of air movers and dehumidifiers and how many days they run. We keep equipment only as long as readings show it is needed.
