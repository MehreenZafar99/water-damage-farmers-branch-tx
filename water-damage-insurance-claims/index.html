Water Damage Insurance Claims in Farmers Branch

A water damage claim goes smoother when you know what your policy looks for and have the right records ready. This guide covers the basics for Farmers Branch property owners and renters.

Is It Usually Covered?

Usually covered

  • A pipe that bursts suddenly
  • A water heater that ruptures
  • A washing machine or dishwasher hose that breaks
  • A roof leak caused by a storm
  • Water from a burst pipe during a freeze

Often not covered

  • A slow leak that went on for weeks or months
  • Damage from skipped maintenance
  • Outside floodwater (needs separate flood insurance)
  • A roof leak from an old, worn roof
  • Mold from a long term leak

The key word insurers use is sudden. Damage that happens all at once is more likely to be covered than damage that builds up slowly.

How the Claim Process Works

  1. Stop the water and protect the property.Policies expect you to prevent further damage.
  2. Call your insurance companyand open a claim. Write down the claim number.
  3. Start drying right away.You don't need to wait for the adjuster to begin water removal.
  4. Meet the adjuster.They inspect the damage and review your records.
  5. Review the estimate.Compare the adjuster's scope with the work that is actually needed.
  6. Complete repairsand keep all final invoices.

How We Help With Your Claim

We photograph the damage, record moisture readings each day, and keep a log of the drying equipment used. You can share these records with your insurer. We can also walk the adjuster through what we found if you would like.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I wait for the adjuster before cleanup?

No. Take photos first, then start water removal. Waiting can make the damage worse, and many policies require you to limit further damage.

Does renters insurance cover water damage?

Renters insurance usually covers your belongings. The building itself is usually the landlord's policy. Let your landlord know right away.

Is flood damage covered by homeowners insurance?

Standard homeowners policies usually do not cover outside flooding. That needs a separate flood policy.

What if my claim is denied?

Ask for the reason in writing and review your policy. The Texas Department of Insurance has consumer help if you have questions about how a claim was handled.
