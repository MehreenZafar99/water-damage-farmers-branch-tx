When a heavy North Texas storm drops a lot of rain fast, water looks for the lowest way in. In Longmires, that can mean water coming under doors, through the garage, or up against the foundation where the yard slopes toward the house. We clean up and dry rain water damage in Longmires homes.

Clean Rain vs Floodwater

Rain that blows in under a door is usually treated like clean water if it's dried fast. Water that ran across the yard, the street, or the driveway picks up soil, fertilizer, and oil. That makes it contaminated, and it needs cleaning and disinfecting, not just drying. Carpet and drywall soaked with outside water are usually removed. See our flood damage page.

Simple Steps Before the Next Storm

  • Replace worn door sweeps and garage door bottom seals
  • Clean sliding door tracks so water can drain out
  • Add downspout extensions to move roof water away from the house
  • Keep gutters clear so they don't overflow at the foundation
  • Keep soil and mulch sloped away from the house
  • Keep valuables in the garage off the floor

What We Do After Rain Water Gets Inside

  1. Extract standing water from floors and carpet
  2. Decide what's clean water and what's contaminated
  3. Remove soaked materials that can't be cleaned
  4. Clean and disinfect where outside water came in
  5. Dry floors, baseboards, and lower walls
  6. Repair and restore

Frequently Asked Questions

Is rain water under my door covered by insurance?

It depends. Rain driven in by a storm through a damaged opening may be covered. Water that rose from the ground is usually treated as flood, which needs flood insurance.

Can wet carpet from rain be saved?

If it was clean rain and dried quickly, often yes. If the water ran across the yard first, it's usually removed.

How do I stop water from coming in under my garage door?

A new bottom seal and making sure the driveway doesn't slope toward the door both help.

Do you fix drainage or grading?

No. We handle the water damage inside. A landscaper or drainage contractor can fix grading.
