A Room by Room Leak Walkthrough

Kitchen. The dishwasher, fridge ice maker line, and sink supply lines all sit hidden in or behind cabinets. Leaks here soak the cabinet base and the floor under it. Look for swollen toe kicks and soft spots in the floor near the dishwasher.

Laundry room. A burst washer hose can release a lot of water in minutes. Water runs under the machine, behind the wall, and into the next room. Braided steel hoses are a cheap upgrade.

Garage. Many water heaters sit in the garage. A tank that gives out can wet the garage walls and anything stored on the floor. If the tank is older than about 10 years, keep an eye on it.

Bathrooms. Toilet supply lines, wax rings, and shower pans leak slowly and often go unnoticed. Watch for loose floor tiles, a rocking toilet, or stains on the ceiling below.

Hall closet or attic. Some homes keep the water heater or AC air handler here. Leaks from these spots drop down through ceilings and into carpet.

Quick Fixes to Avoid a Big Leak

ItemSimple step
Washer hosesSwap rubber hoses for braided steel
Water heaterCheck the age on the label and flush it yearly
Fridge ice maker linePull the fridge out once a year and check the line
DishwasherLook under the sink for drips every few months
ToiletsReplace supply lines that look cracked or bulging
AC drainPour a cup of vinegar in the drain line each month in summer

When a Leak Happens

  1. Shut off water at the appliance valve or the main valve
  2. Unplug the appliance if you can do it from a dry spot
  3. Move belongings away from the water
  4. Take photos
  5. Reach us to extract the water and check for hidden moisture

Frequently Asked Questions

My dishwasher leaked but the floor looks dry now. Is it fine?

Maybe not. Water often runs under cabinets and into the subfloor. We can check with a moisture meter.

Is it safe to use my kitchen during drying?

Usually yes. We place equipment to keep the space usable where we can.

Can water heater leaks damage the garage floor?

Concrete usually handles it well, but nearby drywall, baseboards, and stored items often get damaged.

Are household appliance leaks covered by insurance?

Sudden leaks are often covered. Slow leaks that went on for a long time often aren't.
