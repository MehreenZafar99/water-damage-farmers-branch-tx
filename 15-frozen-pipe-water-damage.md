## Why Frozen Pipes Burst

When water freezes, it expands. That builds pressure inside the pipe between the ice and the closed faucet. The pipe splits at its weakest spot. The leak often doesn't show until the ice thaws, which is why many people discover damage a day or two after the cold passes.

**Pipes most at risk in local homes:**
- Pipes in exterior walls
- Lines in the attic
- Pipes in garages and utility rooms
- Outdoor hose bibs and sprinkler lines

---

## Freeze Warning Prevention Checklist

When a hard freeze is in the forecast:

- [ ] Disconnect garden hoses and cover outdoor faucets
- [ ] Let indoor faucets drip on exterior walls, both hot and cold
- [ ] Open cabinet doors under kitchen and bathroom sinks
- [ ] Keep the thermostat steady, day and night
- [ ] Keep the garage door closed if water lines run through it
- [ ] Know where your main shutoff valve is
- [ ] Shut off and drain your sprinkler system
- [ ] Wrap exposed pipes in the attic and garage with foam sleeves

## Thawing a Frozen Pipe: Do and Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Open the faucet so melting water can flow | Use an open flame or torch |
| Use a hair dryer or warm towels on the pipe | Leave a space heater running unattended |
| Start warming near the faucet and work back | Pour boiling water on the pipe |
| Shut off the main valve if you see a crack | Wait for it to thaw on its own if water is spraying |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How cold does it need to be for pipes to freeze?
Risk rises when temperatures stay below about 20°F for several hours, especially for pipes in exterior walls, attics, and garages.

### Will I know right away if a pipe burst?
Not always. The leak often shows once the pipe thaws. Check your water meter after a freeze with all taps off.

### Is frozen pipe damage covered?
Often yes, if the home was heated and you took reasonable steps. Policies vary, so check yours.

### Should I turn my water off before leaving during a freeze?
If you will be away, shutting off the main valve and draining the lines lowers the risk of a flood while you're gone.
