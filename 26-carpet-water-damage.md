## Can Your Carpet Be Saved?

| Situation | Carpet | Pad |
|---|---|---|
| Clean water, dried within 24 to 48 hours | Usually saved | Often replaced |
| Clean water, wet more than 48 hours | Sometimes saved | Replaced |
| Gray water (washer, dishwasher drain) | Sometimes saved after cleaning | Replaced |
| Sewage or floodwater | Removed | Removed |
| Carpet already delaminating or smelling | Replaced | Replaced |
| Mold growing on the back | Replaced | Replaced |

Carpet pad works like a sponge. It holds a lot of water and is cheap to replace, so replacing it is often the smart move even when the carpet is saved.

---

## What Delamination Means

Carpet has a front face and a backing held together with glue. When it sits wet too long, that glue lets go and the layers separate. You may see ripples or bubbles, or feel the carpet shift underfoot. Once delamination starts, the carpet usually can't be saved.

## How We Dry Wet Carpet

1. **Extract.** Weighted extraction tools press water out of carpet and pad.
2. **Lift and check.** We pull back the carpet to check the pad and subfloor.
3. **Decide on the pad.** Soaked pad is usually removed.
4. **Float and dry.** Air movers blow under the carpet so it dries from both sides.
5. **Clean.** Carpet is cleaned and deodorized after drying.
6. **Reinstall.** New pad goes in and the carpet is stretched back into place.

---

## What You Can Do Right Now

- Move furniture off the wet carpet, or put foil under the legs to stop stains
- Pick up rugs, shoes, and anything sitting on the floor
- Stay off the wet area as much as you can
- Don't use a household vacuum on water
- Don't turn on high heat

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does wet carpet take to dry?
With professional equipment, usually 2 to 4 days.

### Why does my carpet smell after it dried?
The pad or subfloor likely stayed wet, or bacteria started growing. The smell means the job isn't finished.

### Can I dry carpet with fans?
Fans dry the top but leave the pad and subfloor wet. That leads to odors and mold.

### Is carpet water damage covered by insurance?
If the water came from a sudden, covered event like a burst pipe, carpet and pad are often covered.
