## What to Do in the First 10 Minutes

Use this checklist while you wait for our crew.

- [ ] **Stop the water.** Shut the main valve. In most Farmers Branch homes it sits near the front of the house or close to the water meter by the street.
- [ ] **Cut power to wet rooms** at the breaker panel, but only if you can reach it without stepping in water.
- [ ] **Move valuables up and out.** Lift electronics, papers, photos, and rugs off wet floors.
- [ ] **Take photos and video** of every wet area before anything is moved. Your insurance company will want them.
- [ ] **Blot, don't vacuum.** Use towels for small spots. Never use a regular household vacuum on water.

## How Our Restoration Process Works

1. **Inspection.** We find where the water came from and use moisture meters and thermal cameras to see how far it spread, even behind walls and under floors.
2. **Water extraction.** Truck mounted and portable extractors pull standing water out of carpet, pad, and hard floors.
3. **Drying.** Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure. We check moisture levels each day and log the readings.
4. **Cleaning and repair.** We clean and sanitize affected areas, then handle drywall, flooring, and trim repairs so the space is back to normal.

## Why Water Damage Hits Farmers Branch Homes Differently

**Older plumbing.** A large share of homes here were built between the 1950s and 1970s. Aging supply lines, galvanized pipes, and cast iron drains fail more often as they age.

**Clay soil and slab foundations.** North Texas clay swells when wet and shrinks in drought. That movement puts stress on pipes under the slab, which is why [slab leaks](/services/slab-leak-water-damage/) are common here.

**Spring storms and hail.** Heavy rain and hail can crack shingles and let water into attics and ceilings. See our [storm damage](/services/storm-damage-restoration/) page.

**Hard freezes.** The February 2021 winter storm burst pipes across Dallas County. A single hard freeze can do the same. Learn more on our [frozen pipe](/services/frozen-pipe-water-damage/) page.

### How fast can you get to my property in Farmers Branch?
We dispatch crews around the clock. Farmers Branch is a compact city, so travel time is usually short. Call {{PHONE}} and we will give you an arrival window on the phone.

### Do you handle homes and businesses?
Yes. We work on any property with water damage, from apartments and townhomes to houses, offices, and shops.

### How long does drying take?
Most jobs take 3 to 5 days of drying. Hardwood, plaster, and heavy saturation can take longer. We check readings daily and only remove equipment once materials are dry.

### Will my insurance pay for this?
Sudden damage like a burst pipe or broken supply line is often covered. Slow leaks and outside flooding often are not. Our [insurance claims page](/water-damage-insurance-claims/) explains what to expect.

### Can I just use fans and let it dry?
Fans dry the surface but often leave moisture inside walls and under flooring. Hidden moisture leads to mold and odor. Professional drying uses meters to confirm everything is actually dry.

It depends on the size of the area, the type of water, and the materials involved. Our [cost guide](/water-damage-restoration-cost/) breaks it down.
