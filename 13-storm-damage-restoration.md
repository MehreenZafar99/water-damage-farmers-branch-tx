# Storm Damage Restoration in Farmers Branch

Spring in North Texas brings hail, strong wind, and sudden downpours. When a storm opens a gap in your roof, windows, or siding, water finds its way in. We dry and restore storm damaged properties across Farmers Branch and help protect them from the next round of rain.

## After the Storm Checklist

Walk through this once the weather clears:

- [ ] Look at ceilings for new stains, drips, or sagging
- [ ] Check the attic for wet insulation or daylight through the roof deck
- [ ] Look for missing or dented shingles from the ground
- [ ] Check window frames and sills for water
- [ ] Look for water inside at exterior doors
- [ ] Clear gutters and downspouts of leaves and hail
- [ ] Photograph all damage inside and outside
- [ ] Call your insurance company to report the damage

## Stopping More Water From Getting In

A damaged roof keeps leaking with every rain until it is fixed. That's why we start with **temporary protection**, like roof tarps or boarding over broken windows. Then we dry the inside. Your roofer handles the permanent roof repair.

---

## Why Storm Damage Often Hides

Water from a roof leak can travel along rafters and pool far from where it came in. Wet attic insulation holds moisture against the ceiling for days. A small ceiling stain can mean a much bigger wet area above it. Thermal imaging and moisture meters help us find it all.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is hail damage covered by insurance?
Hail damage is often covered under homeowners policies in Texas. Check your deductible, since many policies have a separate one for wind and hail.

### Do you replace roofs?
No. We handle the water damage inside and temporary protection. A roofer handles the roof itself.

### How soon should I call after a storm?
As soon as you see signs of water inside. The next rain can make it worse.

### What if I don't see any damage inside?
Check the attic. Roof leaks often show up there first, days before a ceiling stain appears.
