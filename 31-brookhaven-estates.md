## How Long Home Plumbing Parts Usually Last

Every part in your plumbing has a typical lifespan. In an established home, some may be original.

| Part | Typical lifespan | What failure looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Washer and toilet supply hoses | About 5 to 10 years | Sudden burst or a slow drip behind the fixture |
| Water heater tank | About 8 to 12 years | Rust at the base, then a leak or rupture |
| Shutoff valves under sinks | About 15 to 25 years | Valve won't close fully or drips at the stem |
| Galvanized steel supply pipes | About 40 to 60 years | Low pressure, rusty water, pinhole leaks |
| Copper supply pipes | Often 50 years or more | Pinhole leaks and weak joints |
| Cast iron drain lines | About 50 to 75 years | Cracks, slow drains, sewage backups |

If your home still has original pipes and drains, it's worth having a plumber inspect them.

## Two Problems We See Most

**Supply side leaks.** Old pipes and fittings inside walls start leaking slowly. You may notice a stain, peeling paint, or a spike in your water bill before you ever see water. See [burst pipe water damage](/services/burst-pipe-water-damage/).

**Drain side backups.** Aging cast iron drains crack, and tree roots from mature yards can grow into the joints. The result is slow drains, then a backup. See [sewage backup cleanup](/services/sewage-backup-cleanup/).

## Drying Older Homes the Right Way

Established homes can have materials newer homes don't, like plaster, original hardwood, and older subfloors. These hold water longer and need slower, careful drying. We measure each material and adjust the setup so wood and plaster dry evenly without cracking or cupping.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I replace my old pipes before they fail?
That's a question for a licensed plumber, but an inspection is a smart step if your home still has original galvanized pipes or cast iron drains.

### Can original hardwood floors be saved after a leak?
Often yes, if drying starts quickly and is done slowly and carefully. See our [hardwood floor page](/services/hardwood-floor-water-damage/).

### Why do I keep getting slow drains?
In older homes, it's often buildup or roots in the main drain line. A plumber can check it with a camera before it turns into a backup.

### Is damage from old pipes covered by insurance?
A sudden burst often is. Damage from a slow leak or wear over time often isn't.
