# Mold Remediation in Farmers Branch

Mold needs three things: moisture, something to eat like drywall or wood, and time. North Texas heat and humidity give it a head start. When a leak isn't dried fast, mold can start growing within 24 to 48 hours. We remove mold from water damaged areas in Farmers Branch and fix the moisture problem behind it.

## Signs You May Have Mold

- A musty, earthy smell that won't go away
- Black, green, gray, or white spots on walls, ceilings, or baseboards
- Peeling or bubbling paint
- Warped or stained drywall
- Allergy symptoms that get worse indoors
- A recent leak that was never fully dried

---

## Mold Myths vs Facts

| Myth | Fact |
|---|---|
| Bleach kills mold for good | Bleach can lighten mold on hard surfaces but doesn't fix mold inside porous materials like drywall |
| If you can't see it, you don't have it | Mold often grows inside walls, under flooring, and in attics |
| Painting over mold solves it | Mold keeps growing under paint and comes back |
| Only black mold is harmful | Many types of mold can cause health problems for sensitive people |
| Mold goes away once it dries out | Dried mold can still release spores. It needs to be removed |
| A dehumidifier is enough | It helps prevent growth but doesn't remove existing mold |

## Texas Mold Rules

Texas regulates mold assessment and remediation through the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation. For larger jobs, generally over 25 contiguous square feet, the work is typically handled by licensed mold assessors and licensed remediation contractors. Small areas under that size are often handled as part of water damage cleanup.

## Our Mold Remediation Process

1. **Find the moisture source.** Mold comes back if the leak isn't fixed.
2. **Contain the area.** Plastic barriers and negative air pressure keep spores from spreading.
3. **Filter the air.** HEPA air scrubbers capture spores during the work.
4. **Remove moldy materials.** Drywall, insulation, and carpet with mold growth are bagged and removed.
5. **Clean what can be saved.** Framing and hard surfaces are cleaned and treated.
6. **Dry everything.** Drying equipment brings materials back to normal moisture levels.
7. **Rebuild.** New drywall, insulation, and flooring go in once the area is clear.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I remove a small patch of mold myself?
A small spot on a hard surface can often be cleaned with gloves and a mask. Mold on drywall, in insulation, or over a large area should be handled by professionals.

### Will mold come back after remediation?
Not if the moisture source is fixed and the area is fully dried.

### Is mold covered by insurance?
Mold from a sudden covered leak is sometimes covered, often with limits. Mold from long term leaks usually isn't.

### How long does mold remediation take?
Small jobs can take a day or two. Larger jobs with rebuilding can take a week or more.
