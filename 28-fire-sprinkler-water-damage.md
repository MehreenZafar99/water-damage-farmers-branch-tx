| Who | Their job |
|---|---|

## Why Sprinkler Water Is Often Dirty

Water in sprinkler pipes can sit for years without moving. It often comes out dark, smelly, and full of rust and sediment. That means sprinkler water damage usually needs cleaning, not just drying. It can also stain carpet, ceiling tiles, and furniture.

---

## Common Reasons Sprinkler Heads Go Off

- A ladder, box, or furniture bumps a head
- Something is hung from a sprinkler head
- Pipes freeze in cold spaces during a hard freeze
- A head fails from age or corrosion
- Heat from cooking or equipment near a head

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does one sprinkler head set off all of them?
No. Most systems only activate the heads that are hit or heated. That's why many incidents involve just one head.

### Who pays for sprinkler water damage in an apartment?
It depends on the cause and the leases. Building insurance often covers the structure, and renters insurance covers belongings.

### Why is the water black?
Water sits in sprinkler pipes for a long time and picks up rust and sediment.
