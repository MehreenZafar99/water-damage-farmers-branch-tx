# Slab Leak Water Damage in Farmers Branch

Most Farmers Branch homes sit on concrete slab foundations with water lines running underneath. When one of those lines leaks, water soaks up through the slab into flooring, walls, and cabinets. It often goes unnoticed for weeks. We dry and restore the damage once the leak is found and fixed.

## Why Slab Leaks Are Common Here

North Texas has expansive clay soil. It swells when wet and shrinks during dry spells. That constant movement shifts the foundation and puts stress on the pipes below it. Add older copper or galvanized lines in homes from the 1950s to 1970s, and slab leaks become a common local problem.

## Slab Leak Warning Signs Checklist

Check off anything you notice:

- [ ] A warm or hot spot on the floor, often from a hot water line
- [ ] Damp or wet carpet with no clear source
- [ ] Hardwood or laminate floors lifting or cupping
- [ ] Cracks in tile or grout that keep getting bigger
- [ ] A musty smell near the floor
- [ ] The sound of running water with every tap off
- [ ] A water bill that jumped for no reason
- [ ] Your water meter moving with all water off
- [ ] Your water heater running more than usual
- [ ] Mold or mildew along baseboards

If you checked two or more, call a leak detection plumber and then call us.

## How Slab Leak Restoration Works

**Step 1: Leak detection.** A plumber uses listening tools and pressure tests to find the leak under the slab.

**Step 2: Leak repair.** The plumber repairs the line by digging through the slab or rerouting the pipe through walls or the attic.

**Step 3: Water damage assessment.** We map moisture in the slab, flooring, walls, and cabinets.

**Step 4: Remove what can't be saved.** Wet carpet pad, swollen laminate, and damaged baseboards are removed.

**Step 5: Dry the slab and structure.** Concrete holds a lot of water, so slab drying needs targeted equipment and time.

**Step 6: Restore.** New flooring, baseboards, and drywall go in once readings confirm the slab is dry.

---

## Why Concrete Takes Longer to Dry

A concrete slab soaks up water like a sponge and gives it back slowly. If new flooring goes down over a wet slab, it can warp, grow mold, or fail its adhesive. That's why we test the slab before any flooring goes back in.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do you find and fix the slab leak?
A leak detection plumber finds and fixes the pipe. We handle the water damage and drying after.

### How long does slab drying take?
It often takes longer than a normal leak, sometimes a week or more, depending on how long the leak ran.

### Is slab leak damage covered by insurance?
Some policies cover the water damage and the cost to access the pipe, but not the pipe repair itself. Check your policy.

### Can a slab leak damage my foundation?
Water under the slab can make clay soil swell or wash out, which can add to foundation movement. Fixing the leak quickly lowers that risk.
