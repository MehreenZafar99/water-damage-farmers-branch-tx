## How Flooding Happens in Farmers Branch

North Texas storms can drop a lot of rain in a short time. When that happens, water can back up at storm drains, pool at low points in yards, and push in under doors and garage entries. Properties near Farmers Branch Creek, drainage channels, and low lying streets feel it first. Clay soil also soaks up slowly, so runoff sits longer.

## Flood Safety Checklist

Before you go back inside:

- [ ] Turn off power at the breaker only if you can reach it from a dry spot
- [ ] Do not touch outlets, cords, or appliances that got wet
- [ ] Wear rubber boots and gloves
- [ ] Keep kids and pets out of wet areas
- [ ] Watch for sagging ceilings and warped floors
- [ ] Throw out food that touched floodwater
- [ ] Wash hands well after any contact with the water
- [ ] Take photos before moving anything

## Why Floodwater Is Treated Differently

The industry calls outside floodwater **Category 3 water**. That means it is considered contaminated. Here is what that changes:

- Porous materials that soaked it up are usually removed
- Surfaces are cleaned and disinfected, not just dried
- Crews wear protective gear during cleanup
- Drying only starts after contaminated materials are out

## What Can Be Saved and What Can't

| Usually can be cleaned and saved | Usually has to be removed |
|---|---|
| Tile and sealed concrete floors | Carpet and carpet pad |
| Solid wood furniture | Upholstered furniture that soaked through |
| Metal and glass items | Drywall below the water line |
| Hard plastic items | Insulation inside wet walls |
| Many appliances after inspection | Mattresses and pillows |
| Cabinets that did not swell | Particleboard furniture and cabinets |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does homeowners insurance cover floods?
Standard homeowners policies usually don't cover outside floodwater. That needs a separate flood policy. Read our [insurance guide](/water-damage-insurance-claims/).

### Can I clean flood damage myself?
Small amounts of floodwater still carry germs. Protective gear, disinfection, and removing wet porous materials are needed. For more than a small area, call a pro.

### How soon does mold grow after a flood?
Mold can start within 24 to 48 hours. Contaminated water makes it grow faster.

### Can my drywall be dried instead of replaced?
Not after a flood. Drywall that soaked up floodwater is removed, usually up to a foot or more above the water line.
