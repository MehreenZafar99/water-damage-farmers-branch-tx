# Roof Leak Water Damage in Farmers Branch

A roof leak rarely drips right where the problem is. Water gets in at one spot, runs along the roof deck and rafters, soaks the insulation, and shows up as a stain somewhere else. We track down every wet area in your Farmers Branch home and dry it before mold and ceiling damage set in.

## Where Roof Leaks Usually Start

| Entry point | Why it leaks | Where the water often shows up |
|---|---|---|
| Missing or cracked shingles | Hail and wind damage | Ceiling stains in the middle of a room |
| Flashing around chimneys | Sealant dries out and pulls away | Walls and ceilings near the chimney |
| Plumbing vent boots | Rubber seals crack in the Texas sun | Bathroom and hallway ceilings |
| Roof valleys | Heavy rain collects where two slopes meet | Corners of rooms and along walls |
| Skylights | Worn seals and flashing | Around the skylight frame |
| Clogged gutters | Water backs up under the roof edge | Exterior walls and top of windows |

## Attic Inspection Checklist

If you can safely get into the attic, look for:

- [ ] Dark or wet spots on the underside of the roof deck
- [ ] Damp, matted, or discolored insulation
- [ ] Water trails running down rafters
- [ ] Rusty nails poking through the deck
- [ ] Daylight showing through the roof
- [ ] A musty smell
- [ ] Stains around vent pipes and the chimney

Step only on the ceiling joists, never between them. Drywall won't hold your weight.

---

## Why Wet Insulation Matters

Wet insulation acts like a sponge sitting on top of your ceiling. It holds water against the drywall for days, which leads to sagging and mold. It also stops insulating, so your energy bills go up. Soaked insulation is usually removed and replaced once the attic is dry.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does the ceiling stain appear far from the leak?
Water travels along the roof deck and rafters before it drips down. The stain shows where it finally landed, not where it came in.

### Can I wait until the roof is fixed to deal with the water?
No. Get the tarp on and start drying now. Wet insulation and drywall can grow mold within 48 hours.

### Is roof leak damage covered by insurance?
Leaks caused by a storm are often covered. Leaks from an old, worn roof usually aren't.

### Do you replace roofs?
No. We work on the water damage inside. Your roofer handles the roof repair.
