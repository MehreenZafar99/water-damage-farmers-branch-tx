# AC Leak Water Damage in Farmers Branch

In a Texas summer, your air conditioner pulls gallons of water out of the air every day. That water has to drain somewhere. When the drain line clogs or the pan overflows, it ends up in your ceiling, closet, or floor. We dry and repair AC leak damage across Farmers Branch.

## Why Your AC Is Leaking

| Cause | What happens | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Clogged condensate drain line | Algae and dirt block the line and water backs up | Clear the line and flush it |
| Rusted or cracked drain pan | Water drips through holes in the pan | Replace the pan |
| Frozen evaporator coil | Ice melts all at once and overwhelms the pan | Fix airflow, filter, or refrigerant issue |
| Dirty air filter | Low airflow leads to coil icing | Replace the filter |
| Broken condensate pump | Water can't be pumped out and overflows | Repair or replace the pump |
| Disconnected drain line | Water drains into the ceiling or wall instead of outside | Reconnect the line |

---

## Where AC Water Ends Up

In many Farmers Branch homes, the air handler sits in the attic, a hall closet, or the garage.

- **Attic unit:** Water soaks insulation and drips through the ceiling below
- **Closet unit:** Carpet, baseboards, and walls around the closet get wet
- **Garage unit:** Drywall and stored items near the unit take the hit

Because AC leaks are often slow, they can run for weeks before anyone notices. That makes mold a real risk.

## Summer AC Drain Checklist

Do this once a month from May through September:

- [ ] Change or check the air filter
- [ ] Look in the drain pan under the air handler for standing water
- [ ] Pour a cup of white vinegar into the drain line access to stop algae
- [ ] Check that water drips from the outside drain line when the AC runs
- [ ] Look for stains on the ceiling under an attic unit
- [ ] Test the float switch if your system has one
- [ ] Schedule yearly AC service before summer

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much water does an AC make?
On a humid Texas day, a home system can pull several gallons of water out of the air.

### Should I turn off my AC if it's leaking?
Yes, until the drain is cleared. Running it keeps adding water to the leak.

### Can an AC leak cause mold?
Yes. AC leaks are often slow and hidden, which gives mold time to grow on insulation and drywall.

### Is AC leak damage covered by insurance?
It depends on whether the leak was sudden or ongoing. Slow leaks are often denied, so act fast once you notice one.
