## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do you fix the leak itself?
Our work is the water damage: removal, drying, cleaning, and repair. For pipe or fixture repairs, a licensed plumber handles the leak. We can work alongside your plumber.

### Can you start the same day?
Yes. We run 24/7, so work usually starts on the first visit.

### What if the damage is from more than one source?
We treat it as one job and plan the drying around every affected area.
