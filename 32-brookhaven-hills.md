## How Trees Lead to Water Damage

**Clogged gutters.** When gutters fill with leaves, rain spills over the edge or backs up under the roof line. Water runs down walls and soaks the fascia, soffit, and the tops of windows.

**Branches on the roof.** Limbs that rub the roof wear down shingles. A falling branch in a storm can punch straight through.

**Debris in roof valleys.** Leaves pile up where two roof slopes meet. Water pools there and finds its way under the shingles.

**Roots in drain lines.** Mature roots can grow into older sewer lines and cause backups.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can clogged gutters really cause water damage inside?
Yes. Overflowing gutters send water down the walls and under the roof edge. It often shows up as stains at the tops of walls or around windows.

### A branch hit my roof. What should I do first?
Stay out of rooms under the damage, cover belongings, and call us. We can put a temporary tarp on the roof and start drying inside.

### Is tree damage covered by insurance?
If a tree falls on your home in a storm, the damage to the house is often covered. Check your policy for limits on tree removal.

### Do you clean gutters or trim trees?
No. We handle the water damage inside your home and temporary roof protection.
