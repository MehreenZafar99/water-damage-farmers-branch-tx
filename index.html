How the restoration works

Four stages, the same on every job. You'll know what's happening at each one.

  1. Inspection

    We find where the water came from, then use moisture meters and thermal cameras to see how far it spread behind walls and under floors.

  2. Water extraction

    Truck mounted and portable extractors pull standing water out of carpet, pad, and hard floors.

  3. Drying

    Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure. We take readings every day and log them.

  4. Cleaning and repair

    We sanitize affected areas, then handle drywall, flooring, and trim so the space is back to normal.

Why water damage hits Farmers Branch homes differently

The same leak behaves differently here than in a newer suburb. These four local conditions shape how we inspect and dry every property.

Many homes here sit on concrete slabs with water lines underneath. If a floor feels warm or carpet is damp with no clear source, read our slab leak guide.

Older plumbing

A large share of homes were built between the 1950s and 1970s. Aging supply lines, galvanized pipes, and cast iron drains fail more often with age. See burst pipes.

Clay soil under slab foundations

North Texas clay swells when wet and shrinks in drought. That movement stresses pipes under the slab, which is why slab leaks are common here.

Spring storms and hail

Heavy rain and hail can crack shingles and let water into attics and ceilings. See storm damage.

Hard freezes

The February 2021 winter storm burst pipes across Dallas County. One hard freeze can do the same. See frozen pipes.

Will insurance cover it?

Insurers look for one word: sudden. A pipe that bursts all at once is often covered. A slow leak that ran for weeks often isn't. Outside flooding needs separate flood coverage.

Do you handle homes and businesses?

Yes. We work on any property with water damage, from apartments and townhomes to houses, offices, and shops.

How long does drying take?

Most jobs take 3 to 5 days of drying. Hardwood, plaster, and heavy saturation can take longer. We check readings daily and only remove equipment once materials are dry.

Will my insurance pay for this?

Sudden damage like a burst pipe or broken supply line is often covered. Slow leaks and outside flooding often are not. Our insurance claims page explains what to expect.

Can I just use fans and let it dry?

Fans dry the surface but often leave moisture inside walls and under flooring. Hidden moisture leads to mold and odor. Professional drying uses meters to confirm everything is actually dry.

How much does water damage restoration cost?

It depends on the size of the area, the type of water, and the materials involved. Our cost guide breaks it down.
