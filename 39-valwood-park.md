## How Each Floor Type Handles Water From Below

| Flooring | What water does to it | Can it usually be saved? |
|---|---|---|
| Carpet | Soaks the pad first, then the carpet backing | Carpet often yes if dried fast. Pad usually replaced |
| Ceramic or porcelain tile | Tile holds up, but water sits under it and in the grout | Usually yes. Loose tiles may need resetting |
| Solid hardwood | Swells and cups as it absorbs moisture | Often yes with slow, careful drying |
| Engineered wood glued to the slab | Glue bond can break and boards can lift | Sometimes. Depends on how long it was wet |
| Laminate | Fiberboard core swells and doesn't recover | Usually replaced |
| Luxury vinyl plank | Planks resist water, but water gets trapped underneath | Planks can often be lifted, dried under, and reinstalled |

## The Problem With Water Under Floors

Most floors look fine on top while water sits underneath. Tile and vinyl plank are the worst for this, because they're water resistant on the surface and trap moisture below. Trapped water leads to odor, mold, and loose flooring weeks later. Moisture meters and thermal imaging tell us what's happening under the surface.

## Slab Leaks in Valwood Park Homes

Water lines under the slab can leak as North Texas clay soil shifts through dry and wet seasons. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, damp carpet with no clear cause, cracked tile, or a water bill that jumps. A leak detection plumber finds and fixes the pipe. We dry the slab and floors after.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My vinyl plank floor is waterproof. Why is it a problem?
The planks resist water, but water can get trapped underneath them on the slab. That trapped moisture can cause mold and odor.

### How do you check for water under tile?
With moisture meters and thermal imaging. We can often find wet areas without pulling up any tile.

### How long does it take to dry a slab?
Often longer than other materials, sometimes a week or more, depending on how long the water was there.

### Can I lay new flooring right after drying?
Only after the slab tests dry. Otherwise the new floor can fail.
